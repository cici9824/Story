# Story

### Story 1
A young walking around the ruins in the village. This was originally a rental area, and became a ruin after demolition. An old lady observed the young suddenly and thought he was a thief, so she followed, trying to stop him. The young dragged the old lady to go back, thinking about how to get rid of her. On the way, he came across a shop, and the old lady started to call for help towards the shop staff. He continued dragging her to go forward, and they encountered a deep pit. The young dragged her past it, making her one leg in the pit, with her upper body leaning outside. The shop staff wanted to help,but unexpectedly, the old lady became exhausted, released her hands, fell into the deep pit with a scream.
